Best 4XVR alternative on Quest?

Finally VR Video Player, if you wanted 4XVR for NAS + rips and not for every toggle on earth. Free. Native OpenXR. Guided SMB. Decrypted Blu-ray/MVC. SIMPLE/PRO instead of a wall of controls. Keep 4XVR

If you already paid and it plays the shelf.

Do not uninstall a working 4XVR because a landing page asked you to. The store app is free; Premium is a ~$30-class IAP. Developer copy: 30-day local 2D trial plus 5 minutes per day for 3D, SMB, USB. Install Finally next to it. If a title 4XVR hates plays here — or the other way around — you learned something. Power users often keep both.

Discs

ISO collectors. Finally’s ISO guide is explicit: decrypted rips you already own, no menus, no decryption service. That is the free overlap. For the rest of the library, NAS and VR180.
