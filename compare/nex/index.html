Direct answer

Finally or NEX Video Player?

NEX if you want the fewest settings and will pay in the $10 class for that — USB, PC link, travel, almost no cockpit. The store app is a trial plus Full IAP; the listing is today’s price. Finally if you want that calm for $0, with PRO still in the building when a VR180 is packed wrong. NEX remains the cleaner “almost no settings” paid app. Finally remains the library player with a field manual.

NEX FAQ

Is NEX or Finally better for Quest 3?

NEX is a trial plus Full IAP in the $10 class (USB, PC link, few settings). Finally is free, native, no account, with a SIMPLE cockpit and PRO still available when projection is wrong. Pick NEX if you will pay to hide settings. Pick Finally if you want the same calm plus a real PRO board.

SIMPLE is free. PRO does not cost extra because nothing costs extra.
