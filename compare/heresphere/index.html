Direct answer

Finally or HereSphere?

HereSphere if you live in IPD, distortion, crop, and depth and will pay ~$30 for Unreal-class calibration. Finally if you want the files to play — local + NAS, VR180/360/fisheye with sticky overrides, SIMPLE/PRO, passthrough keying, no account. Finally has placement, convergence, and a lens family. It is not pretending to replace HereSphere’s microsurgery.

HereSphere FAQ

Is Finally like HereSphere?

No. HereSphere is a paid Unreal-class optical bench (depth, distortion, crop, calibration). Finally is a free native OpenXR library player with live placement, lens family, and per-title overrides. Use HereSphere when geometry is the whole hobby.

If the file just needs a lens that sticks, start free.
