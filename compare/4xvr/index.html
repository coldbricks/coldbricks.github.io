4XVR FAQ

Is Finally better than 4XVR?

Different jobs. 4XVR is the paid power-user format board (ISO, USB, WebDAV, dense toggles) with a free store app plus Premium IAP. Finally is free, native, no Finally account, with sticky projection, BASIC/PRO, and its own experimental decrypted Blu-ray/MVC path.

Is 4XVR free or paid?

Check the current 4XVR listing for the download price, Premium options and any trial limits. Availability and terms can vary by store and region.
