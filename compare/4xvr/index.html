Finally or 4XVR?

Buy 4XVR Premium if you already know you want every container toggle and a dense pro board — USB, WebDAV, ISO, the works, at a ~$30-class IAP on a free store app. Install Finally if you want that job in a free native player with a calmer SIMPLE/PRO split, guided SMB, and per-title memory. If 4XVR already plays your shelf, you do not need a speech from us.

The ISO question.

Collectors buy 4XVR for disc images. Finally’s ISO / MVC guide is the free overlap: decrypted rips you already own, no menus, no decryption service. 4XVR still wins people who want the thickest format matrix money can buy.

Questions

4XVR FAQ

Is Finally better than 4XVR?

Different jobs. 4XVR is the paid power-user format board (ISO, USB, WebDAV, dense toggles) with a free store app plus Premium IAP. Finally is free, native, no account, with sticky projection, SIMPLE/PRO, and its own decrypted Blu-ray/MVC path.

Is 4XVR free or paid?

The store app is free. Premium is a ~$30-class IAP. Developer copy: 30-day trial of local 2D files, plus 5 minutes per day for 3D, SMB, USB, and other advanced paths. The listing is today’s price.

Try Finally on the same rips. The store listing is free.
