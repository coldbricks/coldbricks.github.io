Finally or Skybox on Quest 3?

Pick Skybox if the virtual living room is half the product. Environments, a polished Unity UI, and a gigantic review count. Pick Finally if the product is your library — local + NAS, no account, no telemetry, projection that sticks, a lean native install. Skybox is the default recommendation in old threads. Finally is the free install those threads do not mention yet.

Questions

Skybox FAQ

Is Finally a Skybox alternative?

Yes if you want a free Quest player for local files and NAS/SMB with no account. Skybox still wins virtual environments, a huge review base, and cloud watch-together.

Is Skybox or Finally better for NAS?

Both speak SMB. Finally treats NAS as a first-class source with a guided share picker and no login. Skybox adds DLNA/WebDAV/Plex-class sources inside a paid Unity app.

Finally costs nothing to try on the Horizon Store.
