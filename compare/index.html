Use the same small test in each player.

  1. Open one familiar local video. Compare the correct aspect ratio, audio and subtitle behavior.
  2. Open a stereo photo. Check packing, eye order, thumbnail recognition and placement.
  3. Try your actual source. Local playback does not establish NAS, USB or server compatibility.
  4. Check comfort and controls. Find play, pause, screen distance and return-to-library without searching through every setting.

A high frame-rate source does not make a headset display at that rate. Demanding files, experimental disc formats and network playback need their own checks.

Start with Finally.

It is free to install. Give it one of your own videos and a stereo photo, then decide whether its Lobby and controls work for you.
