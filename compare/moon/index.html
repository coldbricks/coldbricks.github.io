Finally or Moon VR Player?

Moon VR if you want a small paid cinema app with a picture reputation and you already live in that ecosystem. Passthrough 2.0 is a listing headline. Moon VR FAQ

Is Moon VR or Finally better?

Moon VR is a paid straightforward cinema player with Passthrough 2.0. Finally is free, native, no account, built around local + NAS libraries with per-title projection memory and SIMPLE/PRO. NEX and Moon share lineage — compare those two as cousins, Finally as the free third option.

If the movie is already on the headset or the NAS, Finally does not need a receipt.
