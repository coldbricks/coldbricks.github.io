HereSphere alternative on Quest?

Finally VR Video Player, if you installed HereSphere to play files and got lost in calibration. Free native OpenXR. Lens family, eye swap, distance, convergence, sticky per-title overrides. NAS and local. Passthrough chroma. No Coldbricks telemetry. HereSphere still wins people who want Unreal-class optical microsurgery and will pay ~$30 for it. Finally vs HereSphere.
