Direct answer

Can Finally play 8K on Quest 3?

Yes — a hardware decoder ceiling up to 8K on Quest 3. That is a decode-path claim, not a promise that every 8K file on a weak Wi-Fi hop will be butter, and not a Quest 2 panel claim. Local 8K is the clean test. NAS 8K is a network test. Finally shows buffer seconds, fill, and MB/s when a stream stalls so you can see which it is. Free. No Coldbricks telemetry.

8K from a NAS.

Wired NAS. 5 or 6 GHz to the Quest. No guest isolation. NAS buffer in GLOBAL SETTINGS (LEAN → MAX) is for this. If the rebuffer banner is the show, copy the title local or lower the bitrate — do not blame the lens. Setup: NAS · Synology · local.

Can Quest 3 play 8K VR video?

Finally VR Video Player has a hardware decoder ceiling up to 8K on Quest 3. Whether a specific 8K file plays smoothly depends on codec, bitrate, and — for NAS — Wi-Fi. Local storage removes the network from the problem. Quest 2 is a supported listing, not an 8K panel claim.

8K belongs on the headset, not in a cloud queue.
