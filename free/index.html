Price

Free.

Finally VR Video Player is free on the Meta Horizon Store. Listed as Early Access. No subscription. No account to create. No ads in the cockpit. Optional support links live on the web manual, not in the player. You do not pay to play files you already own.

Skybox, 4XVR, HereSphere, NEX, and Moon VR are paid apps. They can be worth it. They are not required to watch a folder on a NAS. See alternatives.

What we collect

No Coldbricks telemetry.

The privacy policy is the map. Nothing goes to Coldbricks automatically. Optional Submit Bug Report creates a ZIP locally and sends it only after you press Send. Meta entitlement is pass/fail. Nearby discovery, Watch Together, and other optional network paths are listed there.

  • Library index, resume positions, projection overrides — on the headset.
  • SMB / Plex / WebDAV credentials — on the headset, sent only to the server you named. Plex Direct Play is a user-supplied local PMS URL + token; no cloud login, no transcode.
  • Watch Together — private LAN between two headsets. Coldbricks receives no room.

Meta still does a store entitlement check on store builds. That is Meta, not us.

Catch

There isn’t a catalog funnel.

The “catch” is the product: you bring the files. Finally does not rent you a streaming library. If you wanted a catalog, you are in the wrong app — and that is intentional. Quest 3 overview · NAS setup.

FAQ

Price and privacy questions.

Is there a free VR video player for Meta Quest?

Yes. Finally VR Video Player is free on the Meta Horizon Store, listed as Early Access. No subscription, no account, no ads.

Does Finally collect data or telemetry?

Finally does not send library, viewing, or credential data to Coldbricks automatically. Optional Submit Bug Report sends a ZIP only if you press Send. Meta store builds check entitlement with Meta (pass/fail). Library index and credentials stay on the headset. Other optional network paths are listed in the privacy policy.
