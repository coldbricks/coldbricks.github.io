02 · cockpit

Built for the headset.

Native C++ OpenXR theater — no heavy game-engine wrapper. SIMPLE for daily transport; PRO when you want the whole board. 318+ built-in skins plus a live custom color-wheel builder.

  • SIMPLE and PRO cockpits — everyday transport in SIMPLE; fine eye alignment, 4-key chroma, A–B–C–D cascade loops, parametric EQ, and live telemetry in PRO.
  • True scene dolly & 6DoF parallax — move closer without wrecking world scale. Point at any lit slider and click the stick to snap that control back to its factory detent.
  • Lie-back tilt follow & 3DoF fallback — keep the picture upright to your face in bed, and keep watching even if room lights go out and position tracking drops.
  • Refresh-rate matching (AFR) — cadence-aware display hints for 24 / 25 / 30 / 50 / 60 fps content without fake motion interpolation.
  • Grab the vault and flick it — analytical compositor scroll with variable speed (SLOW to TURBO) and automatic hold acceleration.
  • Subtitles that live on the picture — embedded or sidecar SRT / VTT / ASS, freely draggable anywhere in 2D space with MOVE SUBS.

05 · sources & vault

Your library, your network, your rules.

Local storage, USB-C SSDs, SMB shares, and DLNA servers are first-class citizens — never buried behind a streaming storefront. Saved playlists, offline downloads to headset, and in-VR file copy/move/rename included.

  • Local storage & direct USB-C SSDs — FAT32 and exFAT drives stream multi-tens-of-gigabytes 8K VR files through a direct 512 KiB buffered USB reader.
  • SMB / NAS — instant one-folder browse (no forced whole-share crawl), resumable indexing, and a persistent 64–512 MiB byte reservoir.
  • Zero-config DLNA / UPnP (1.0.43) — auto-discovers Windows 11 Media Streaming, Universal Media Server, MiniDLNA, and Jellyfin on Wi‑Fi and streams 50 GB+ raw VR titles via Direct Play out of the box.
  • WebDAV, Plex Direct Play, Stash, XBVR & FTP — connect your own servers directly; credentials stay encrypted on your headset.
  • Watch Together — synchronize two headsets on a private LAN with mutual TLS 1.3 and short-code verification, zero cloud relay.

Private by construction. No telemetry, analytics, ads, location permissions, or account. Optional Submit Bug Report builds a privacy-safe ZIP locally and sends nothing unless you press Send. Read the privacy policy.

09 · questions

Daily-use FAQ

What is Finally, in one sentence?

A native OpenXR VR video, 3D photo, and music cinema for media you already own — local files, USB-C SSDs, NAS shares, and DLNA servers, with no catalog and no account.

How do I watch a NAS / SMB or DLNA library?

Open SOURCES in the Lobby. For SMB, enter your server/share once and choose Browse instantly. For DLNA (Windows 11 Media Streaming, Universal Media Server, MiniDLNA, Jellyfin), open DLNA — Finally auto-discovers your server on Wi‑Fi and Direct Plays 50 GB+ VR files with zero custom config. Full walkthrough in the connection guide.

Does it play VR180, 360, SBS, fisheye, and 3D photos?

Yes. Filename tags and frame geometry pick a lens; overrides stick per title. Stills share the same vault with sampled SMB/WebDAV/FTP thumbnails, 360° panorama viewing, beat-synced slideshows, and customizable PHOTO STICK modes.

Is it free? Can I support development?

Finally is completely free on both the Meta Horizon Store and Google Play — no ads, no subscriptions, no locked PRO features. If you want to buy the developer a coffee, optional external tip links (PayPal, Cash App, Venmo) live in the web field manual, never inside the headset app.

What do you collect?

Nothing automatically, to anyone. Finally requests zero location permissions and never accesses the headset microphone. Library records, credentials, and playback memory stay on your hardware. Optional Submit Bug Report builds a redacted ZIP locally and sends nothing unless you press Send. Read the privacy policy.

Which headsets are supported?

Meta Quest 3, Quest 3S, Quest Pro, and Quest 2 on the Meta Horizon Store, and Samsung Galaxy XR on Google Play.
