A few things to know.

Do I need a subscription or a Finally account?

No. Finally is free to install and does not require a Finally account. Your headset's store account is used to obtain the app.

Does Finally upload my collection?

Local and SMB playback do not upload your media to Coldbricks. Finally has no app analytics or advertising. Optional sources, support sharing and platform services have their own data paths, explained in the privacy policy.

Will every file play?

No player can promise that. Codec, bitrate, resolution, source frame rate and network conditions matter. Start with a file you know works; see the manual for supported routes and limitations. Blu-ray/MVC is experimental and requires decrypted media you have the right to use.

Can I use the same guides on Galaxy XR?

The core player is shared, but install steps and some headset behavior differ. Start with the Galaxy XR guide. Quest refresh-rate or performance results are not Galaxy guarantees.
